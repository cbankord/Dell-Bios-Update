Behavioral limits intentionally documented in README.md and OPERATIONS.md:
- No resident controller or exact-date scheduling. Intune supplies the next
  invocation; reminder hours are a cooldown, not a guaranteed notification time.
- Deadline starts at the first active-user prompt launch ATTEMPT, not proof of
  visible display or reading; launch failure/retry cannot reset it.
- No forced reboot countdown. Only explicit Restart Now requests the guarded
  managed restart. Monitor staged updates and outstanding owned suspension.
- File tattoo/hash identify version and integrity, not publisher authenticity.
  The reviewed package and enterprise signing policy establish runtime trust.
  After signing runtime scripts, regenerate both manifest and Intune detection.
- New files use ProgramData\Medela\DellBIOS. Old protected ProgramData diagnostic
  records and firmware transaction identity remain for recovery continuity.
